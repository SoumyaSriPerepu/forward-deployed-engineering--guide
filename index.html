Introduction

Why this blog exists

Forward Deployment Engineering is where software engineering meets real-world problem solving. Forward Deployed Engineers work closely with customers, understand their operational challenges, and build systems that create measurable value.

The work may involve integrating APIs, cleaning complicated data, deploying cloud applications, debugging production systems, or building AI solutions around a customer’s unique workflow.

I created this blog to document my journey into this field. I will share what I learn about software systems, cloud infrastructure, generative AI, data engineering, system design, deployment, and customer-focused product development.

My goal is not only to study these technologies, but to understand how they work together to solve problems outside a classroom or coding interview.
